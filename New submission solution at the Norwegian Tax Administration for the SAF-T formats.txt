A new submission solution at the Norwegian Tax Administration for the SAF-T formats (SAF-T Cash Register and SAF-T Financial) is under development and ready for testing!

Would you like to participate in testing the new solution? If so, please send me an email at ingerid.robole@skatteetaten.no, and I will invite you to the Slack channel that has been set up for testing the new solution.
In the Slack channel, you will among other things find guidance on how to test the submission of SAF-T files to the Norwegian Tax Administration, and you can ask questions or provide input on the new submission solution.
Developers are connected to the channel and will be available to answer any questions about the solution.
